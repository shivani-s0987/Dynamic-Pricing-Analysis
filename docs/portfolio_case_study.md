# Portfolio case study

## Problem
A retailer needs a repeatable way to monitor changing prices, competitor gaps, product-level volatility, simulated demand and gross profitability.

## Approach
Generated a fixed-seed dataset spanning 18 months, implemented a cleaning/quarantine pipeline, defined reproducible price and profit metrics, used median matched competitor benchmarks, created analytical SQL, generated CSV/Markdown/PDF outputs, and built a Streamlit interface with six pages including a what-if simulator.

## Demonstrated capabilities
Pandas ETL, data-quality controls, SQL joins and window functions, descriptive analysis, interactive Plotly charts, scenario mathematics, report generation, tests and documentation.

## Limitations
All records are synthetic. Results illustrate analytical workflow only and do not claim actual market intelligence or experimentally proven business outcomes.
